# Records and Arkansas FOIA review checklist

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice. Arkansas counsel and applicable City policy owners must review it before live use.

The initial schema marks every case `PENDING_LEGAL_REVIEW` and implements no automatic deletion. That is a safety default, not an approved retention determination.

- [ ] Determine which records constitute public business in every operating mode.
- [ ] Approve retention schedules for cases, communications, appointments, RSVPs, drafts, audit events, exports, and backups.
- [ ] Define legal-hold authority and make holds override ordinary deletion.
- [ ] Define who may search, review, redact, export, approve, and release records.
- [ ] Approve a redaction workflow without encoding assumed statutory exemptions in software.
- [ ] Preserve originals, revisions, source links, export manifests, and audit history.
- [ ] Separate constituent-service, public content, administrative, campaign, and private-business systems and exports.
- [ ] Decide how official-business communications outside the platform are captured.
- [ ] Define response-time tracking and escalation without auto-releasing records.
- [ ] Review vendor contracts, data locations, subprocessors, breach terms, and preservation capabilities.
- [ ] Confirm what happens to records and access when office status changes.
